# PRISM Guide for partners

Nov 27, 2026 · @felcha

## Introduction

PRISM is the CRAF'd platform for your project documents, reports and project updates. You fill them in online, submit them to the CRAF'd Secretariat, and answer CRAF'd's comments in the same place.

In PRISM you:

- **Complete your Project Document (ProDoc).** It describes your project, its work plan, indicators, risks and budget. It must be approved before funds are released.
- **Submit Project Reports.** An Annual Report for each year and a Final Report at the end of the project.
- **Submit Project Updates.** When CRAF'd opens one for you, for example for a budget revision, a no-cost extension or a tranche release.
- **Answer comments.** CRAF'd may comment on parts of your documents. You reply and update your text.

For questions about content or deadlines, contact the CRAF'd Secretariat at crafd@un.org.

## Getting started

PRISM runs in your browser (Chrome, Edge, Firefox or Safari); there is nothing to install. Your organization has one shared login.

### Logging in

1. **First time.** CRAF'd sends your organization a secure link by email. Open it and set a password. The link is valid for 7 days.
2. **After that.** Go to the PRISM login page and sign in with your organization's username (or email) and password.
3. **Password rules.** At least 8 characters, with an uppercase letter, a number and a special character.
4. **Locked out.** After 5 wrong attempts the account is locked. Contact crafd@un.org to have it unlocked or reset. "Forgot password" does not send an email.

If someone sets a new password, everyone else using the login is signed out.

### Staying signed in

After 15 minutes without activity, PRISM signs you out. One minute before, a message appears: click **Stay signed in** to continue. Your work is saved as you go, so nothing is lost.

### The sidebar

| Item | What it's for |
| --- | --- |
| Home | Your projects, timeline and next steps |
| Project Document | Your project documents and their tabs |
| Project Report | Your Annual and Final Reports |
| Project Update | Project updates CRAF'd has opened for you |
| Project Contacts | Your organization's contacts |
| Guide | This guide |

Project Document, Project Report and Project Update only appear when you have one. Inside a document, its tabs are listed in the sidebar.

To get more space, click the panel icon at the top of the sidebar to collapse it, and the same icon to open it again. Your organization's name and the sign-out button are at the bottom.

## Home

Home shows what needs your attention. The top line sums it up, for example "2 projects · 3 things to do · next deadline 30/11/2026".

Each project has its own box. Click the box header to open or close it. A project with an overdue item is marked **Overdue** in red. Projects that have ended with nothing left to do move to **Past projects** at the bottom.

### In each project box

- **Requested / Grant.** The funding amount. It says "Requested" until your Project Document is approved, then "Grant".
- **Period.** Start and end dates, and the number of months.
- **Open comments.** CRAF'd comments still waiting for your reply.
- **Next deadline.** In dark yellow when it is within 14 days, in red when it has passed.

### The timeline

The timeline shows your Project Document, reports and updates in date order, then the project end, the Final Report and closure.

| Dot | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Green ✓ | Approved, or the date has passed |
| Grey clock | With CRAF'd for review |
| Yellow | In progress; the due date shows below |
| Empty circle | Not started yet |

### Your next steps

The list under the timeline tells you what to do next. Your Project Document comes first, then overdue items, then the rest by due date.

- **Continue** opens a document you are completing.
- **Revise** opens a document CRAF'd has sent back to you.
- Documents with CRAF'd are listed as "With CRAF'd for review". There is nothing for you to do on them.
- When nothing is waiting for you, the list says "Nothing to do right now."

## Project Document

The Project Document (ProDoc) is your project's core document. CRAF'd must approve it before funds are released. It has seven tabs, listed in the sidebar. A tab gets a green tick when it is complete.

| Tab | What you fill in | Needed for the tick |
| --- | --- | --- |
| General Information | Project name, requested funding (USD), start date, duration in months, geographic scope, description (max. 500 words), Gender Equality Marker, project partners, implementing organizations, contacts | All fields except partners, organizations and contacts |
| Narratives | One answer per question | Every answer, max. 500 words each |
| Work Plan | Outcomes, outputs under each outcome, activities under each output, and the quarters each activity takes place | At least one activity, with its outcome and output |
| Indicators | Baseline and target for each indicator, and the outcomes or outputs it measures | A baseline, a target and a link for every indicator; every outcome and output has at least one indicator |
| Risk Management | Risks to the project | Each risk has a description, likelihood and impact |
| Budget | Annual budget per category, a description per category, and the tranche release schedule | Budget and tranche totals match the requested funding |
| Documents | Supporting files | At least one file, or "No documents to upload" ticked |

### Tips per tab

- **General Information.** Fill this in first. **Project partners** are the organizations that receive CRAF'd funds directly; they become the rows of the tranche schedule. If a partner is missing from the list, contact CRAF'd. **Implementing organizations** can be typed in freely. Use the information icons for guidance on each field.
- **Contacts.** Link people from your Project Contacts, or add new ones, and give each a role: Primary focal point, Alternate focal point or Signatory. You can change contacts later.
- **Work Plan.** Items are numbered automatically: 1 (outcome), 1.1 (output), 1.1.1 (activity). Hover the information icons for what each level means.
- **Indicators.** The standard CRAF'd indicators are already listed and cannot be removed. Click **Add indicator** for your own: it needs a name, a description and a means of verification, and you choose whether it is reported annually or at closure.
- **Risk Management.** Click **Add risk** below the table, fill in the row and click **Save**. Likelihood runs from Rare to Very likely, impact from Insignificant to Extreme. The risk level is calculated for you.
- **Budget.** Enter the budget per category and year. Indirect support costs (7%) are added automatically and count towards the total. The total must match the requested funding: up to $1 under, never over. A message shows how far you are off.
- **Tranche release schedule.** Below the budget, enter how much each project partner receives per tranche, with a release date and the activities it covers. A release date is required for every amount. The schedule total must also match the requested funding.

### Submitting the Project Document

When every tab has a green tick, click **Submit** in the top bar and confirm. The document is then locked while CRAF'd reviews it. PRISM does not send an email: please let CRAF'd know at crafd@un.org that it is ready.

## Project Reports

You submit an Annual Report for each year of the project and a Final Report at the end. Both have the same 13 sections, split into Qualitative and Quantitative. Open a report from **Project Report** in the sidebar or from Home.

Text fields have a word limit, shown under each box (most are 100 words).

| Section | What you fill in | Needed for the tick |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Overview | Read the project details and the authorization statement | "I authorize this report" ticked |
| Survey | A rating from 1 to 5 for each question, and an explanation of your rating | A rating and an explanation for every question |
| Results | Cases where your data informed decisions: context, data-driven decision, resulting impact | At least 3 results, each with its context |
| Key Achievements | Up to 3 main achievements and why they matter | At least 1 achievement |
| Partnerships | Partner organizations and the result of each partnership | Every row has an organization, or "No partnerships to report" ticked |
| Lessons Learned | Up to 5 lessons: category, what you learned, what you changed | At least 1 lesson, with all three fields |
| External Coverage | The 3 most relevant pieces of coverage or engagement | 3 descriptions |
| Testimonials | A quote from your leadership; optional quotes from partners or users; optional photos | Leadership quote with author and title; every photo has a caption and credits |
| Work Plan | The status of each activity this year | A status for every activity |
| Indicators | The value achieved and a status for each indicator | A value and a status for every indicator |
| Budget | Actual expenditure per category, and transfers to implementing partners | At least one expenditure above 0, and transfers filled in or "No transfers to report" ticked |
| Risk Management | What has changed for each risk; new risks | See below |
| Complementary Funding | Other funding the project received | Every row has a contributor and an amount, or "No complementary funding to report" ticked |

### Section details

- **Work Plan.** Each activity shows its planned quarters. Pick a status. If it is Delayed, At risk or Suspended, a second row opens to mark the updated quarters and add an explanation.
- **Indicators.** Baselines and targets come from the Project Document and are shown for reference. Annual Reports use the statuses On track, Off track and Ahead of schedule; the Final Report uses Achieved and Not achieved.
- **Budget.** The approved budget and the category descriptions come from the Project Document and cannot be changed here. Enter what was actually spent. Transfers to implementing partners are listed below the budget table.
- **Complementary Funding.** For in-kind contributions (for example services or staff time), give an estimated value in USD.
- **Testimonials.** Photos are optional. If you add one, give it a caption and credits.

### Risk Management

The risks from your Project Document and earlier reports are listed with their last rating. For each one, answer **Since the last report, this risk…**:

| Answer | What to fill in |
| --- | --- |
| has not changed | Nothing more |
| is more or less likely | The new likelihood and impact, and how you are adjusting your mitigation |
| has happened | The impact, and what happened and what you did |

To add a new risk, click **Add risk**. A new risk needs a name, a description, a likelihood and an impact.

### Submitting a report

1. Complete every section until it has a green tick.
2. On **Overview**, tick "I authorize this report".
3. Click **Submit** in the top bar and confirm.
4. Email crafd@un.org to let CRAF'd know it is ready. PRISM does not send an email.

If Submit is greyed out, the message next to it tells you which section to complete first.

## Project Updates

A Project Update is how you request a change to your project, such as a budget revision, a no-cost extension, a tranche release or reprogramming. To request one, contact CRAF'd at crafd@un.org. CRAF'd then opens the update in PRISM with only the sections that apply, and you fill it in there.

The update appears under **Project Update** in the sidebar and on Home. Its name shows the year and the type of change, for example "2027-BR" for a budget revision.

### What you fill in

- **Justification.** Why you are requesting the change, what has changed and why it is needed (max. 100 words).
- **The unlocked sections.** For example Work Plan, Budget or Risk Management. They work like the same sections in a report. A budget revision adds columns for the revised budget; an extension adds the new years and quarters.
- **HACT Recommendations** (if shown). The findings and recommendations from CRAF'd's assurance activities. You only fill in the **Partner comment** to describe the action you have taken.

An approved update changes your Project Document. Past reports stay as they were.

### Submitting an update

When every section has a green tick, click **Submit** in the top bar and confirm. No authorization is needed. Then email crafd@un.org to let CRAF'd know it is ready.

## Statuses and submitting

Every Project Document, report and update has a status. It is shown on the list pages and decides whether you can edit.

| Status | What it means | Can you edit? |
| --- | --- | --- |
| To complete | You are filling it in | Yes |
| Under review | You have submitted it and CRAF'd is reviewing it | No |
| To revise | CRAF'd has sent it back with comments | Yes |
| Approved | CRAF'd has approved it | No |

The usual path is: To complete → Under review → Approved. If CRAF'd needs changes, the document goes to To revise; you update it, answer the comments and submit again.

On the Project Document, Project Report and Project Update pages, use the filter buttons at the top (All, To complete, Under review, To revise, Approved) to show only documents with that status.

### Submitting

- **Submit** appears in the top bar while a document is To complete or To revise.
- It stays greyed out until every section has a green tick. The message next to it names the section to complete.
- Reports also need "I authorize this report" ticked on the Overview section.
- After you confirm, the document is locked for editing.
- PRISM does not notify CRAF'd. Please email crafd@un.org to say it is ready for review.

When a document is locked, a banner at the top says it is with the CRAF'd Secretariat for review, or that it has been approved. Contact CRAF'd if changes are needed.

## Comments

CRAF'd reviewers can leave comments on a section or on a specific item, such as a risk or an indicator.

### Where you see them

- On Home: the **Open comments** tile, and "N comments to address" under the next step it belongs to.
- Under the section heading in the document.
- As a comment icon next to the item. It is yellow while the comment is open and grey once you have addressed it.

### What you can do

1. **Read the comment** and update your content if needed.
2. **Reply** to explain what you changed or to answer a question. You can edit or delete your own replies.
3. **Mark as addressed** when you have dealt with it. You can undo this.

CRAF'd then reviews your reply and resolves the comment. Resolved comments disappear from your view. You can reply to comments even while a document is locked, but you cannot start a new comment yourself.

Tip: keep replies short and say what you changed, for example "Updated the baseline to 2024 data."

## Project Contacts

**Project Contacts** lists the people at your organization. Each contact shows the projects they are linked to and their role on each.

- **Add a contact.** Click **Add contact** below the list. Name, organization and email are required; job title is optional.
- **Edit or delete.** A change here updates the contact everywhere it is linked.
- **Link to a project.** Contacts are linked to a project, and given their roles, in the Project Document's General Information tab.

| Role | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Primary focal point | CRAF'd's main contact for the project |
| Alternate focal point | The contact when the primary focal point is unavailable |
| Signatory | Signs the Project Document on behalf of your organization |

Keep contacts up to date when people join or leave.

## Saving, word limits and completion

### Saving

PRISM saves automatically as you type. The top bar shows "Saving…" and then "All changes saved". Some tables (such as new risks or contacts) have their own **Save** button.

### One person editing at a time

Only one person can edit a document at a time. Because your organization shares one login, this can be a colleague. If someone else has it open, you see "{name} currently has this report open" and the page is read-only.

After 10 minutes without activity, your editing session ends so others can work. One minute before, you can click **Keep editing**. To continue after it has ended, click **Start editing**.

### Word limits

Limited text boxes show a counter, for example "45/100 words". If you go over, the counter turns red. Your text is still saved, but the section does not get its green tick until you shorten it.

### Green ticks and progress

- A section gets a green tick in the sidebar when it is complete.
- The list pages show a progress bar for each document: the share of sections complete.
- You can only submit when every section has a tick.

## Printing

You can print or save a PDF of a Project Document, report or update at any time.

1. Open the document.
2. Click **Print** in the top bar. A print view opens.
3. In your browser's print dialog, choose **Save as PDF** to save the file, or pick a printer.

The PDF contains every section with its current content. The text stays selectable and searchable.

To print this guide, click **Print Guide** at the top of the Guide page.

## Glossary

| --- | --- |
| PRISM | The CRAF'd platform for project documents, reports and updates |
| Project Document (ProDoc) | Your project's core document. It must be approved before funds are released |
| Annual Report | The report you submit for each year of the project |
| Final Report | The report you submit at the end of the project, due 6 months after the end date |
| Project Update | A request to change the project, such as a budget revision, no-cost extension, tranche release or reprogramming |

| Indicator | A measurable value that tracks progress, with a baseline and a target |

| Complementary funding | Other funding the project received, in cash or in kind |

| HACT | CRAF'd's assurance activities; findings may appear in a Project Update |
| Focal point | Your organization's main contact for CRAF'd on a project |
| Signatory | The person who signs the Project Document for your organization |
| Green tick | Shows a section is complete |

## Frequently asked questions

| Question | Answer |
| --- | --- |
| Why can't I edit a field? | The document may be Under review or Approved, a colleague may have it open, or the field comes from the Project Document or is managed by CRAF'd (for example approved budgets, standard indicators and baselines). |
| Why is Submit greyed out? | A section is not complete yet. The message next to the button names it. For reports, also tick "I authorize this report" on Overview. |
| A section won't get its green tick. | Check the required fields, and that no text is over its word limit (the counter turns red). |
| Does CRAF'd know I've submitted? | Not automatically. Please email crafd@un.org after you submit. |
| I work on several projects. How do I switch? | Each project has its own box on Home, and each document is listed in the sidebar. |
| A project partner is missing from the list. | Project partners are added by CRAF'd. Contact crafd@un.org. |
| How do I change something after submitting? | Contact CRAF'd. They can send the document back to you as To revise. |
| Who do I contact for help? | The CRAF'd Secretariat at crafd@un.org. For a technical problem, include a screenshot and a short description. |
